# Personal TickTick APK · Approved Product Design

The approved source specification is `docs/specs/v1-personal-ticktick/`.

## Approved design

- Android 15 personal-use app with online persistence and no login UI.
- Screenshot-faithful dark Today and Month pages.
- Full-screen seven-cell Week grid, with Sunday spanning the bottom row.
- Shared half-height create/edit sheet.
- Single-day all-day, timed, and multi-day tasks.
- Deterministic overdue and recurring-task lifecycle.
- Exact-reminder capability reporting and MagicOS permission guidance.
- Fuzzy title search including Chinese character subsequences and pinyin initials.

## Scope boundary

No task detail page, priority, list/category system, collaboration, habits, focus timer, third-party calendar, account system, or cross-device synchronization.

## Review status

Approved in conversation on 2026-10-02. Subsequent approved additions: multi-day tasks, seven-day Week grid, overdue presentation, and fuzzy title search.
